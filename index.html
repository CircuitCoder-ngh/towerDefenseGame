HOW TO PLAY

    

    

      
OBJECTIVE

        
Malware streams along the glowing data bus toward your CORE. Every leak costs integrity. Survive every wave to secure the sector.

      
DEPLOY

        
Tap a defense in the dock, then tap a dark tile. Tap the same tile again to confirm. You can also drag a defense straight onto the map.

      
MANAGE

        
Tap a tower to upgrade it (3 levels), sell it (70% refund) or change its targeting: FIRST · LAST · STRONG · CLOSE.

      
WAVES

        
Tap START / NEXT to send a wave. Calling a wave early while malware is still on the bus pays bonus bits. Clearing a wave pays a bonus plus Crypto Miner income.

      
ABILITIES

        
EMP stuns everything and strips shields. PURGE calls an orbital strike on a zone you tap. OVERCLOCK boosts every tower's fire rate.

      
THREATS

        
ARMOR reduces each hit, so use Snipers, Lasers and Bombs. CLOAKED malware needs a detector: Sniper, Hub Lv2+ or Arc Lv3. Kill Patchers and Aegis Drones first.

      
RATINGS

        
Finish with 90%+ integrity for 3 stars, 50%+ for 2. Clearing a sector unlocks the next one and ENDLESS mode.
